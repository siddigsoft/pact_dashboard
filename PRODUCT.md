# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Field staff, finance, and super admins use PACT day to day under programme pressure: approvals, field payments, MMP, accounting, HR, and access control. They are mid-task, often on imperfect connections, switching between hubs. Success is finishing the job without hunting through decorative chrome.

## Product Purpose

PACT is the programme operations console for field and finance work. It exists so roles can see the right pages, move money and data through controlled workflows, and audit what happened. Success looks like calm, trustworthy screens where baselines and exceptions are obvious, and visual noise never competes with the task.

## Brand Personality

Calm, institutional, serious. NGO/programme gravity over consumer polish. Voice is plain and bilingual when it helps the work; never cute, never salesy.

## Anti-references

- Purple / blue SaaS glow and gradient accent kits
- Hero-metric card grids (big number + tiny label + accent stripe)
- Navy + gold fintech chrome as default personality
- Card sprawl: nested cards, bordered boxes for every block, banner stacks
- Decorative side stripes, glassmorphism, and invented affordances for standard tasks

Bilingual EN/AR copy that earns its place is allowed and preferred where it helps field/finance users.

## Design Principles

1. **Task over theatre** — Every chrome element must speed a real ops job or leave.
2. **Institutional calm** — Serious programme tone; restraint beats flourish.
3. **One hierarchy** — One primary action per view; density through structure, not card grids.
4. **Bilingual as content** — Keep meaningful EN/AR; never as ornamental filler.
5. **Familiar tools** — Standard nav, tables, forms, tabs; earn trust like Linear/Stripe ops surfaces, not landing pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Default solid accessibility is enough: keyboard focus, readable contrast, semantic controls. No extra WCAG certification mandate for this pass. Prefer reduced-motion friendly transitions (state only, 150–250ms).
