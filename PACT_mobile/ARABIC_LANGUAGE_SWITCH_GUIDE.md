# 🌍 How to View Arabic Translations - Quick Start Guide

## ✅ ARABIC IS 100% COMPLETE - Just Switch the Language!

All Arabic translations are **already implemented** in the code. You just need to switch the app language to see them.

---

## 🚀 **EASIEST WAY: Click the Language Toggle Button**

### New Feature Just Added! 🎉

A language toggle button has been added to the **top-right corner** of the Complete Visit screen.

**Steps:**
1. Open the **Complete Visit** screen
2. Look at the **AppBar (blue/orange top)** 
3. Click the button in top-right showing:
   - **"ع"** (Arabic letter) when app is in English → switches to Arabic
   - **"EN"** when app is in Arabic → switches to English

**That's it!** The entire screen will switch to Arabic with all labels, buttons, and messages displayed in Arabic.

---

## 📱 **Alternative: Change Device Language**

1. **Android/iOS Settings** → Language & Region → Select **العربية (Arabic)**
2. App will auto-detect and display all text in Arabic

## 🔄 Troubleshooting

### If you don't see Arabic after clicking:
1. **Hard refresh the browser**: 
   - Windows: `Ctrl + Shift + R` or `Ctrl + F5`
   - Mac: `Cmd + Shift + R`
   - Firefox: `Ctrl + Shift + R`

2. **Clear app cache**:
   - DevTools → Application → Clear Storage → Clear All

3. **Restart the app** after language change

### If the toggle button doesn't appear:
- Scroll to the top of the screen
- Button is in the **AppBar** (colored header bar at top)
- It shows "ع" (Arabic) or "EN" (English)
