⚙️ What admins configure per work type

Form & Checklist — custom data collection form, required fields, evidence types, and scoring rules per work type

Add-On Modules — MDM, WHM, and other monitoring layers toggled per project — no additional systems needed

Fee & Compensation — flat rate, multiplier (×2), or per-cycle (÷7) — set per activity and work type independently

Approval Chain — one or two-tier approval (Supervisor → Admin) based on project-specific requirements

Team & Hub Assignment — each work item assigned to a specific hub, supervisor, and enumerator with GPS zone matching

Reporting Template — each work type generates its own analytics view, export format, and client-specific report layout

Communication Platform · منصة الاتصالات

When every app is banned,
we built our own.

لأن معظم التطبيقات محظورة في السودان — بنينا قناة اتصالات خاصة بنا ومشفرة وآمنة

🚫 The Sudan Communication Reality

WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, and most third-party communication apps are restricted or intermittently blocked in Sudan. This created a critical operational gap — field teams had no reliable, secure channel to coordinate with supervisors and management. PACT's response: build an integrated communication platform from the ground up, embedded directly in the same system field teams already use.

💬

Encrypted Messaging

One-on-one and group messaging between enumerators, supervisors, and admins — end-to-end encrypted and hosted within PACT's own infrastructure

📞

Voice Calls

In-app voice calling between team members — works over data connections without relying on any third-party platform or carrier restrictions

📢

Broadcast Center

Admins send bilingual announcements to all users, by role, or by hub — with urgency levels, action links, and full delivery tracking

🔔

Smart Notifications

Real-time in-app alerts, push notifications on mobile, and bilingual email digests — all tied directly to field events and approvals

🤖 AI Analysis Engine

Anomaly Detection — flags unusual patterns in field reports: duplicate GPS coordinates, implausible visit times, or identical photo submissions across different sites

Predictive Coverage Alerts — forecasts which states or localities are at risk of falling below coverage targets before the cycle ends

Questionnaire Intelligence — identifies response clusters, outliers, and trends in survey data — surfaced as visual charts and summary cards in the dashboard

Market Price Intelligence — tracks commodity price trends across survey sites, flags significant deviations, and correlates with distribution activity data

Performance Scorecards — per-hub and per-enumerator AI scoring based on consistency, quality, and timeliness across all work types

All reports are available in English and Arabic, exportable to PDF and Excel, and accessible to authorized roles only — with role-based data filtering so each stakeholder sees only what they need.
