The Challenge · التحدي

Field monitoring was
fragmented & manual

كانت المراقبة الميدانية مجزأة ويدوية

📂

Scattered Data

Site visit records spread across spreadsheets, emails, and paper forms with no central source of truth.

💸

No Financial Visibility

Transportation advances, expenses, and enumerator fees tracked manually with no audit trail.

📍

No Real-Time Field Status

Supervisors had no live view of which sites were visited, pending, or missed in the field.

🔄

Slow Approval Cycles

Payment approvals and MMP verifications required back-and-forth emails causing delays.

02 / 08

The Solution · الحل

One platform. Web + Mobile.

منصة واحدة. ويب وهاتف معاً.

📋

Structured Monitoring Plans

Upload monthly site lists, auto-detect activity types and flags, assign to field teams instantly.

📍

Live Field Tracking

Enumerators complete visits on mobile with GPS, photos, and activity reports — online or offline.

💳

End-to-End Financial Control

Advances, expenses, and wallet credits managed with two-tier approval and full audit trail.

📊

Real-Time Dashboards

Coverage gaps, financial alerts, and performance scorecards visible at every management level.
