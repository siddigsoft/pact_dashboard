Dear PACT Team,

We are excited to officially launch the PACT Command Center — our centralized digital platform designed to manage everything from projects and daily tasks to contracts, payroll, and field operations in one place.

To enable full automation of HR and financial processes — including contract management and payroll runs — we need all staff to be registered and active on the platform. Please follow the steps below to create your account today.

👤
Your Role: Employee All staff should register with the Employee role. Your account will be reviewed and activated by the administration team. Managers and directors will be assigned elevated roles by the system admin after registration.
📋 Registration Steps
1
Open the Platform On any browser (Chrome recommended), go to:
https://app.pactorg.com
2
Click "Sign Up" On the login page, click the Sign Up or Create Account link below the login form.
3
Enter Your Work Email & Password Use your official PACT email address. Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters, include numbers and symbols).
4
Select Role: Employee When prompted to select your role, choose Employee. Do not select any other role — the system admin will upgrade roles for managers as needed.
5
Complete Your Profile Fill in your full name, department, and job title. Upload a profile photo if you wish. This helps your manager assign tasks and approve requests correctly.
6
Wait for Activation Your account will be reviewed and activated by the admin team. You will receive a confirmation email once your account is ready.
7
Log In & Explore Once activated, log in and explore your personal dashboard. Start with My Tasks and My Board to see what has been assigned to you.
🚀 What You Can Do on the Platform
📋
My Tasks View, update, and complete tasks assigned to you. Add comments, attach files, and log your daily output with proof uploads.
🗂️
Projects See all projects you are part of. Track progress, view milestones, and collaborate with your team in a structured workflow.
📌
My Board A personal Kanban board showing all your tasks across projects. Drag cards between columns (To Do → In Progress → Done) to stay organized.
⏱️
Timesheets Log your daily work hours. Timesheets feed directly into payroll calculations so accuracy is important.
📄
Contracts & Payroll View your employment contract, track payment history, and download payslips — all digitally within the platform.
🏖️
Leave Requests Submit leave applications, check your leave balance, and track approval status in real time.
📊
Reports & Analytics Access dashboards relevant to your work. See your task completion rates, project health, and field operation summaries.
🔔
Notifications Receive real-time alerts for task assignments, approvals, deadlines, and important announcements — in-app and via WhatsApp.
⚡ Immediate Priority Actions
Once registered, please complete the following right away:
  • Confirm your profile information (name, department, position, phone number)
  • Check for any tasks already assigned to you in My Tasks
  • Inform your line manager that your account is active
  • Contract and payroll processing will begin once all staff are on board — register by the deadline communicated by your manager
🏢 For Managers & Directors

Once your team is registered, you will be able to:

👥
Team Management Assign tasks to team members, monitor workload, and track completion rates from your Team Monitor dashboard.
✅
Approvals Approve leave requests, timesheets, expense submissions, and procurement requests — all in one place with a full audit trail.
💰
Payroll & Contracts Run payroll cycles, review staff contracts, approve salary changes, and generate payslips — automated once all staff are registered.
📈
Portfolio Dashboard Cross-project KPI overview: budget utilization, milestone tracking, project health scores, and risk alerts — all in real time.

Role Who Gets It Key Permissions

Finance Admin Finance team Full accounting suite — GL, budgets, procurement, reconciliation, donor reporting
