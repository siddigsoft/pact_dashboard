02 — Problem Statement
Field operations face
compounding complexity

التحديات المتزايدة في العمليات الميدانية — ما يحله مركز القيادة

📋

Fragmented Planning

MMP and programme data scattered across Excel, WhatsApp, and email. No single source of truth for site coverage, visit status, or team assignments across hubs.

🔀

Disconnected Data Collection

ODK, KoBoToolbox, MoDa, and manual submissions produce separate datasets with inconsistent site names, IDs, and geographic references — impossible to reconcile.

📡

Offline Gaps

Field teams in remote locations (North Darfur, South Kordofan, Blue Nile) operate with intermittent connectivity — standard platforms fail completely without internet.

💸

Manual Financial Reconciliation

Bank receipts processed manually — 5+ minutes each, Arabic OCR errors, no duplicate detection, no audit trail. Incompatible with USAID, EU, UNOCHA donor requirements.

📉

No Data Quality Control

No systematic validation of submitted data — fake GPS coordinates, incomplete questionnaires, duplicate submissions, and unverified photos accepted without challenge.

👁️

Zero Real-Time Visibility

Programme managers cannot see live coverage, team locations, forming bottlenecks, or financial position — decisions made on information that is days or weeks old.

Save sessions & export

Named sessions in DB, append across days, export Excel with 2 sheets, per-account export.
