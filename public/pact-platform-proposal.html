02 — Problem Statement
Sudan field operations
face unique compounding challenges

التحديات الفريدة للعمليات الميدانية في السودان

📋

Fragmented Planning

MMP data scattered across Excel files, WhatsApp groups, and email chains. No single source of truth for site coverage, visit status, or team assignments.

💸

Manual Financial Reconciliation

Bank transfer receipts processed manually — 5+ minutes per receipt, high Arabic OCR error rates, no duplicate detection, no audit trail for donors.

📡

Connectivity & Offline Gaps

Field teams in remote locations (North Darfur, South Kordofan, Blue Nile) operate with intermittent or zero internet — standard web tools completely fail.

👁️

No Real-Time Visibility

Programme managers have no live view of covered sites, field team locations, forming bottlenecks, or real-time financial position.

📑

Compliance & Audit Risk

Donor requirements (USAID, EU, UNOCHA) demand structured records, geographic coverage evidence, quality scores, and timestamped approvals — impossible on paper.

🔄

Complex Multi-Role Workflows

Operations involve 8+ distinct roles — Super Admins, Hub Managers, Coordinators, Data Collectors, Finance Officers — with no shared system for tracking handoffs.

19 — Manual: Transaction Scanner
Scanner Step-by-Step
Manual & Tips

دليل ماسح الإيصالات — خطوة بخطوة

FULL WORKFLOW
1

Navigate to Transaction Scanner

Admin menu → Transaction Scanner (top of left sidebar). Route: /admin/transaction-scanner

2

Upload Screenshots

Drag & drop or click "Add Images". Supports PNG, JPG, WebP. Bank of Khartoum (green) and Bankak styles both supported.

3

Process with AI

Click "Scan All". Gemini 2.0 Flash processes each image. If daily quota is reached, Llama 4 via Groq takes over automatically.

4

Review Extracted Data

Check all 9 fields per row. Amber-highlighted rows = duplicate Transaction ID. Review carefully before export.

5

Delete Problem Rows

Click the trash icon on any row to remove a misread or unwanted receipt. Does not affect other rows.

6

Save Session

Click "Save Session" → give it a name (e.g., "March WFP Transfers") → saved permanently to database for future access.

7

Export to Excel

Click "Export Excel" → downloads formatted .xlsx with 2 sheets: Full Transactions + Recipients Summary.

PRO TIPS
  • Upload up to 30 images at once for batch processing
  • "Add More Images" appends to current session any time
  • "Export by Account" groups by recipient account number
  • Session history: access any past session from the sidebar
  • AI failover is silent — you will not see an interruption
  • Bankak screenshots: ensure text is not blurred for best results
COMMON ISSUES & FIXES
  • Wrong Arabic name: Row-level delete, re-upload cleaner screenshot
  • Duplicate detected: Review both rows — one may be a test transfer
  • Amount = 0: Image may be too small — use full-resolution screenshot
  • Session not saving: Refresh page; session data is preserved in browser until saved
EXCEL SHEET STRUCTURE
  • Sheet 1 — Transactions: ID, Date, From, To, Name, Mobile, Comment, Amount
  • Sheet 2 — Summary: Unique recipients, total amount per account, count
  • Duplicate rows marked amber in Sheet 1
