Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PACT collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the PACT Mobile application and the PACT Command Center web platform (together, the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect account details (name, email, phone, employee ID, role), GPS location (with permission), site visit and operational data, photos and documents, messages and call metadata, device and app diagnostics, push notification tokens, and optional biometric login usage processed on your device only.

2. How We Use Information

We use data to operate the Services, authenticate users, assign and verify field work, process financial workflows, send notifications, improve reliability, and meet legal obligations.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data with authorized users in your organization, service providers (such as cloud hosting, authentication, messaging, and analytics providers), and when required by law.

4. Security & Retention

We use technical and organizational safeguards including encryption in transit and access controls. Data is retained as needed for operations, financial records, and legal compliance.

5. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or export of your data, and you may withdraw consent for optional features such as location sharing.
