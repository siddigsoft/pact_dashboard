---
name: Country Director MMP oversight
description: Business rule for Country Director visibility versus MMP workflow actions.
---

Country Directors have organization-wide read and Full Report visibility across MMPs even when they are not assigned as members of individual projects.

**Why:** The Country Director is an oversight role. Requiring project-team membership caused the MMP page to appear empty despite valid page and read permissions.

**How to apply:** Exempt Country Directors from project-membership filtering for MMP lists and reports. Do not use this exemption to grant upload, forwarding, assignment, approval, cycle-close, or deletion actions.
